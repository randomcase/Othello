# The Liturgical Commune of Othello

## Architecture of the Rule of Hours

Chartered Under the King (His Majesty King Charles III via `0xCROWN_WARRANT_OTHELLO_SCRIPTORIUM`) and sworn in garrison service to the Doge and Senate of Venice, time inside Othello is governed by six daily Canonical Hours and three seasonal observances that bind choir, mill, foundry, and scriptorium together:

1. **Vigils (3rd Hour Before Dawn)** — Twelve cantors of the *Choir of the First Watch* hold alternating bronze stalls so psalmody never drops into silence before daybreak.
2. **Lauds (First Light)** — Lampblack ink is ground in stone mortars by the *Scriptorium of the Ash-Ink*; the morning Synod docket is read aloud.
3. **Prime (Sunrise)** — Tools, reed pens, and labor pairs are allotted without hierarchy for the morning watch.
4. **Sext (High Noon)** — All tools rest. Residents share unleavened loaves and pass salt in silence while wax-tablet grievances are laid beside the Steward's bowl.
5. **Vespers (Dusk)** — The *Order of Bell-Founders* tolls the evening bronze; written grievances are read publicly once midday heat has cooled.
6. **Compline (Nightfall)** — Reliquaries are shuttered, lamps are trimmed to one sanctuary wick, and the *Hermits of the Sealed Marginalia* lock the outer apocrypha chests.

## The Seven Categories of Commune Lore

Every submission brought to the first **Liturgical Commune Lore Form** belongs to one of seven recognized divisions:

- **Canticle_Or_Hymn** — Antiphonal psalms, bell-chants, and night-watch melodies sung across stalls.
- **Canon_Precept** — Binding rules of shared tables, labor allotments, fasts, and silent reconciliation.
- **Rite_Or_Sacrament** — Ceremonies of vow-taking, bread-breaking, ash-grinding, or sanctuary sealing.
- **Hagiography_Or_Martyr** — Lives, trials, and final utterances of remembered founders and watchkeepers.
- **Relic_Or_Vestment** — Consecrated bronze, ash-wood shrines, shrouds, iron spoons, and foundry artifacts.
- **Commune_Chronicle** — Historical annals of synods, famines, fires, schisms, and reconciliations.
- **Apocrypha_Or_Heresy** — Suppressed marginalia, omitted stanzas, and unorthodox claims kept under seal.

## Synod Triage Lifecycle

Submissions move from the intake form into `Lore_Submissions`, undergo schema and citation verification via automated CI, and receive one of four Synod dispositions:

- **Pending_Synod** — Newly inscribed lore awaiting reading at Lauds.
- **Canonized** — Ratified into Tab III of the World Codex and recited during its designated hour.
- **Disputed** — Retained in public memory with marginal glosses contesting its witness.
- **Sealed_Apocrypha** — Preserved under wax lock by the Hermits of the Sealed Marginalia rather than erased.
