# Othello — World Charter & Overview

## World Premise

**Othello** is a dark-parchment worldbuilding setting anchored by the **Liturgical Commune**: an austere Venetian garrison-commune chartered Under the King (`0xCROWN_WARRANT_OTHELLO_SCRIPTORIUM`) and sworn to the service of the Doge and Senate of Venice, where bell-foundries, salt-granaries, reed-cells, and ash-ink scriptoria braid survival, service, and devotion into a single continuous Rule of Hours.

Rather than separating theology, labor, and civic law, every citizen of Othello belongs to an observed hour, an iron refectory measure, and a living ledger of canticles, precepts, relics, and sealed apocrypha.

## Aesthetic Pillars

- **Liturgical Craft**: Brass tuning forks, lampblack ink ground from burnt parchment trimmings, cracked bronze bells, and salt-cellars passed without speech.
- **Communal Equality Under Vow**: Abbot and novice dine with identical iron spoons at Sext; disputes are written anonymously on wax tablets rather than shouted in halls.
- **Canon vs. Sealed Apocrypha**: No lore vanishes quietly. Disputed hymns and dangerous relics remain indexed under Synod seal inside the outer reed-cells.

## Authoring & Canon Conventions

1. Every lore entry receives a sequential canonical identifier (`LC-001`, `LC-002`, ...) upon entering `Lore_Submissions`.
2. Authors assign one **Liturgical Category**, one **Canonical Hour or Season**, and an optional **Epigraph or Antiphon** that can be chanted or inscribed on stone.
3. Entries begin in `Pending_Synod` until the Scriptorium of the Ash-Ink ratifies them as `Canonized`, flags them `Disputed`, or locks them as `Sealed_Apocrypha`.
